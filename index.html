Research Interests研究方向

  • Data-efficient medical vision: I develop self-supervised and few-shot learning methods that capture subtle pathological cues from limited and weakly labeled data, aiming to build reliable diagnostic models when expert annotations and abnormal samples are scarce.
  • Multimodal disease intelligence: I study how visual appearance, temporal behavior, and epidemiological dynamics can be modeled together, designing efficient spatiotemporal architectures and data-driven models for disease detection, health monitoring, and transmission analysis.
  • 数据高效医学视觉：我致力于研究自监督与小样本学习方法，从有限、弱标注的数据中捕捉细微的病理特征，在专家标注和异常样本稀缺的条件下构建可靠的智能诊断模型。
  • 多模态疾病智能：我关注如何联合建模视觉外观、时序行为与疾病传播动力学，并设计高效的时空学习架构和数据驱动模型，用于疾病识别、健康监测与传播分析。
